Why it fails — and the caveats

  • Transport didn't beat ICL. Stack's native in-context learning is the strongest method on Δ-Pearson (0.30) and DE-LFC (0.70); MMD steering trails slightly and has worse energy distance.
  • No Stack representation advantage on correlation. Under a matched ridge decoder, Stack and PCA tie on Δ-Pearson; Stack wins only the distributional energy-distance check (−2.55).
  • Two donors, descriptive only — no population-level significance; per-unit std ≈ 0.18, so gaps of −0.01 to −0.03 sit within noise.
  • MMD is un-tuned (fixed rank 8 / α 1) rather than the contract §9 donor cross-fit — the most likely lever to move the Q1 verdict, and the recommended next step.
  • DE-LFC underpowered — the control-only panel leaves only 10/18 units with ≥10 significant DE genes (median deg_count = 3).
